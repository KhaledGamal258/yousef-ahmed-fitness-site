# Design choice — October 2026

One website with one content library (video, tips, recipes), not two near-identical themes. Material 3-inspired custom shape and state tokens; no claim of using an official Expressive web library. Sources: https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview and https://m3.material.io/styles/shape/overview-principles . This is a suitability judgment for this brand, not a universal ranking of design systems.

Contact: stable pill, no transform/bounce, 52–56px target, visible keyboard focus, hover background/shadow, reduced-motion support and footer clearance. Removed redundant business number/username block. Three alternatives available in components.html. All changes remain local.
